# Privacy Policy for Parlay-Pal

**Effective Date:** November 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 10, 2026

Welcome to **Parlay-Pal** ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the Parlay-Pal mobile application (the "App"), how that information is used and shared, and the privacy choices available to you.

---

## 1. Nature of the App (Statistical Research & Analytics Only)
Parlay-Pal is a sports statistics visualization tool, historical player trend analyzer, and mathematical probability calculator. **Parlay-Pal is NOT a sportsbook or gambling operator.** The App does not accept real-money deposits, process financial wagers, issue payouts, collect banking or payment card details, or facilitate real-money gambling transactions.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, register, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to use Parlay-Pal.
* **Simulated Slip Inputs:** Player prop selections and simulated wager numbers entered into the SGP calculator are stored locally on your device state and transmitted transiently to our calculation endpoint solely to compute mathematical probabilities and expected value metrics. These inputs are not linked to any personal identity.
* **Support Inquiries:** If you contact us directly via email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message solely to respond to your inquiry.

### B. Information Collected Automatically Through Third-Party SDKs
To keep Parlay-Pal 100% free to use, the App displays advertisements served by **Google AdMob** and retrieves sports analytics data via **Google Firebase**. Depending on your device permissions and regional consent choices, these services may automatically collect:
* **Device and Advertising Identifiers:** Such as Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS (only if you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) and the Google Advertising ID (AAID) on Android, along with app-scoped installation identifiers.
* **IP Address and Coarse Location:** Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general, non-precise geographic region (country/state/city level) for regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, and contextual ad delivery. Parlay-Pal does **not** request or collect precise GPS location.
* **Ad Interaction and Usage Data:** Ad impressions viewed, ad clicks, session activity, and basic feature interaction signals used for ad frequency capping, ad performance measurement, and fraud detection.
* **Diagnostics and Device Metadata:** Device model, operating system version, language setting, crash logs, and app performance metrics used to maintain stability and compatibility.

---

## 3. How Information Is Used
Information processed through the App and its integrated SDKs is used to:
1. **Deliver Core App Functionality:** Retrieve player prop hit rates, game matchup projections, and Gaussian Copula correlation calculations via Google Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions.
2. **Serve and Measure Advertisements:** Display banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob, cap how frequently the same ad appears, measure ad effectiveness, and detect invalid traffic or ad fraud.
3. **Personalize Ads (Where Permitted):** Deliver interest-based advertisements tailored to your preferences **only** when permitted by your iOS App Tracking Transparency selection and applicable regional consent choices. Where consent or tracking permission is declined, only non-personalized contextual ads are served.
4. **Maintain Security and Performance:** Monitor server uptime, diagnose crashes, and protect backend endpoints against abuse.

## 5. Your Privacy Rights & Controls

### A. iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT)
On iPhone and iPad devices, Parlay-Pal requests permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for cross-app ad personalization.
* If you tap **"Ask App Not to Track,"** AdMob will not access your IDFA and will serve non-personalized ads.
* You can change or revoke your choice at any time on your device under **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Parlay-Pal**.

### B. Android Advertising ID Controls
On Android devices, you can opt out of personalized advertising or reset/delete your Advertising ID at any time under **Settings → Google → All services → Ads**.

### C. European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland (GDPR)
If you access the App from the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland:
* **Legal Basis:** Personalized advertising and non-essential device storage rely on your explicit **consent** collected via Google's User Messaging Platform (IAB TCF framework) at app launch. Basic app delivery, security, and fraud prevention rely on **legitimate interests** or technical necessity.
* **Your Rights:** You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of personal data, restrict or object to processing, and **withdraw your consent** at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

### D. United States State Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA & Applicable US State Laws)
Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Florida, Oregon, and other US states with comprehensive privacy laws have the right to:
* Know what categories of personal data are collected, used, or shared.
* Request deletion of personal information (note that Parlay-Pal maintains no user accounts or direct personal profiles).
* **Opt Out of "Sale," "Sharing," or Targeted Advertising:** Use of third-party advertising identifiers for personalized ads may constitute "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under state privacy laws. You may opt out via the in-app US State Privacy prompt (where presented) or at any time by disabling tracking in your iOS/Android system settings as described above.
* **Non-Discrimination:** You will receive full access to Parlay-Pal's features regardless of your privacy or tracking selections.

## 6. Age Restriction & Children's Privacy (18+ Only)
Parlay-Pal is intended strictly for adult sports fans aged **18 years or older** (or **21+** where applicable in your jurisdiction). We do not direct the App to children under 13 (or minors under 18) and do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

## 7. Data Retention & Security
* **Parlay-Pal Records:** Because we do not maintain user accounts, we do not store personal user profiles on our servers. Local parlay slip selections remain solely in your device memory until you remove them or uninstall the App.
* **Third-Party SDK Logs:** Google AdMob and Firebase retain server, diagnostic, and advertising log data according to Google's standard retention schedules and anonymization policies.
* **Security:** All network communication between the App, Firebase, Cloud Functions, and AdMob is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
